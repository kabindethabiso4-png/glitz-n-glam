Our Policy

Booking

All appointments require a R100 deposit fee.

Cancellation

Cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment so we can fill the slot. Cancelling outside the 24-48 hour window without proper communication means a 30% charge of the booked service.

Pricing

All pricing is subject to change. We will inform all clients of any price change.

No Show

We are willing to move things around and accommodate in some circumstances, as long as there is communication. Applies to new and existing clients.
